# Bank Reconciliation Statement | Excel

A bank reconciliation for a sample trading company, **Apex Global Trading Ltd**, for October 2026.

## What was done
- Every cash book (general ledger) entry matched against the bank statement by cheque and reference number
- Reconciling items identified: deposit in transit, uncleared cheques, a direct EFT receipt and bank service charges

## Result
After adjusting for the 5,100 deposit in transit, 27,200 of uncleared cheques, the 3,100 EFT receipt and 150 of bank charges, **bank and book balances agree at 36,200**.
